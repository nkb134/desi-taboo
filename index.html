      How to play

      1. Split into teams. One player is the clue-giver each round; the rest of their team guesses.
      2. Describe the word at the top of the card without saying it, or any of the 5 taboo words below it. No rhymes, no "sounds like", no spelling!
      3. Got it ✓ = +1 point. Taboo ✕ (you said a banned word) = −1. Pass ↷ = 0 points, but free passes are limited. You can also swipe the card: right = got it, left = taboo, up = pass.
      4. Opponents watch the phone and press Taboo if the clue-giver slips up.
      5. The team with the most points after all rounds wins. Full paisa vasool!
